About

As data-driven technologies become increasingly pervasive in critical domains, challenges such as fairness, robustness, interpretability, and data quality are central to trustworthy deployment. RDI provides an interdisciplinary forum connecting data mining, data management, and AI to support reliable, transparent, and accountable decision-making.

The session welcomes contributions spanning algorithmic foundations, system design, evaluation methodologies, and domain applications.

Topics

  • Fairness and bias detection, evaluation, and mitigation
  • Explainability and interpretability of data mining and AI models
  • Transparency, accountability, and data/model provenance
  • Data quality assessment, data cleaning, and dataset curation
  • Robust and reliable data mining under noisy, incomplete, or adversarial data
  • Responsible data pipelines and end-to-end system reliability
  • Trustworthy LLMs, RAG, and AI-assisted analytics with human-in-the-loop
  • Agentic AI and responsible intelligent systems
  • Graph, relational, spatial, temporal, and multimodal data mining with reliability considerations
  • Responsible applications in finance, ESG, healthcare, smart cities, and industry
