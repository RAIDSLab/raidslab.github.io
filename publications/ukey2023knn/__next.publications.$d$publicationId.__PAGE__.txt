This operation plays a crucial role in various domains, including knowledge discovery, data mining, similarity search applications, and scientific research. However, exact kNN search in high-dimensional spaces is computationally demanding, and existing sequential methods face challenges in handling large datasets. In this paper, we propose an efficient parallel solution for dynamic kNN join over high-dimensional data, leveraging the high-dimensional R tree (HDR Tree) for improved efficiency. Our solution harnesses the power of Simultaneous Multi-Threading (SMT) technologies and Single-Instruction-Multiple-Data (SIMD) instructions in modern CPUs for parallelisation. Importantly, our research is the first to introduce parallel computation for exact kNN join over high-dimensional data. Experimental results demonstrate that our proposed approach outperforms the sequential HDR Tree method by up to 1.2 times with a single thread.
