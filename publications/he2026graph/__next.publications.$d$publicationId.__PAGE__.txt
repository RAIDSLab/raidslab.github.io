Although many reachability indexes have been proposed, existing empirical studies remain limited in benchmark diversity, evaluation dimensions, and practical deployment guidance. In this work, we present a comprehensive empirical study of representative reachability indexes across diverse graph structures, query workloads, and both static and dynamic settings. We construct a large-scale benchmark covering 1,400 graphs and more than 38,000 query configurations, significantly broadening the structural and workload coverage of prior evaluations. To enable a more practical comparison, we introduce a unified multi-metric evaluation framework with complementary metrics that jointly consider query time, index size, construction cost, and dynamic update behaviour, thereby capturing a richer range of space-time trade-offs than previous studies.
